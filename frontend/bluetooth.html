Web Bluetooth Technical Scope & Android Bridge

• Browser Restrictions: Web Bluetooth is strictly limited to BLE GATT Client operations with a packet MTU of 20–512 bytes. It cannot transfer large multi-megabyte files or act as a Bluetooth Classic RFCOMM serial socket.
• Native Android Solution: For high-speed Classic Bluetooth binary file transfer without router infrastructure, ProxiLink includes a native Kotlin module located in android/BluetoothFileTransferService.kt with standard SPP UUID (00001101-0000-1000-8000-00805F9B34FB) and OBEX profile support in android/BluetoothOBEXTransferService.kt.
